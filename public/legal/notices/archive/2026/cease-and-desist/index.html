PUBLIC NOTICE — CEASE AND DESIST DEMAND

Jurisdiction: State of Georgia, United States of America

Issued by: an unnamed resident of the State of Georgia, referred to below as “the Protected Party” (identity withheld from this publication and preserved in the sealed issuance record).

Effective: immediately upon publication of this notice and, as to any person who otherwise obtains knowledge of it, immediately upon that knowledge.

Nature of this notice: This is a formal private demand notice. It is not a court order, and it does not claim to be one. It states, in permanent and unambiguous terms, that consent is withheld and withdrawn as set out below, so that no person can later claim mistake, misunderstanding, implied permission, or ignorance of that fact.

1. DEFINITIONS

In this notice:

  • “Person” means any individual, group, organization, company, agency, or entity, of any kind, wherever located, and includes every agent, employee, contractor, volunteer, associate, successor, and proxy of that Person.
  • “Protected Parties” means the Protected Party; the Protected Party’s spouse; the Protected Party’s offspring, including minor children; members of the Protected Party’s household; and the Protected Party’s immediate family.
  • “Conduct” means any act, omission, attempt, preparation, direction, encouragement, assistance, or facilitation, whether direct or indirect, in person or remote, by hand or by any device, system, account, service, or technology now known or later developed.
  • “Contact” means any communication or attempted communication in any form, and any act directed at a Protected Party that is intended, or reasonably likely, to be perceived by, reach, affect, or gather information about that Protected Party.
  • “Signal” means any signal, data, electric current, tone, sound, vibration, light, radio-frequency, electromagnetic, ultrasonic, microwave, or other energy or transmission of any kind, whether or not perceptible to human senses.
  • “Device” means any telephone, computer, account, network, camera, sensor, vehicle or vehicle system, smart-home or security system, tracker, or other equipment or service used by, belonging to, or associated with a Protected Party.

2. WITHDRAWAL AND DENIAL OF CONSENT

The Protected Party withdraws, denies, and withholds consent — permanently — to every act prohibited by Section 3. No consent shall ever be inferred from silence, delay, non-enforcement, prior acquaintance or relationship, the absence of a reply, the passage of time, or any other circumstance. Consent, if it is ever given for a specific act, is valid only if given in advance, in a writing signed by the Protected Party, identifying that specific act.

3. DEMAND TO CEASE AND DESIST

General prohibition. Every Person is directed to immediately and permanently cease and desist from all Conduct directed at any Protected Party that communicates with, monitors, watches, follows, investigates, tracks, interferes with, threatens, harasses, intimidates, coerces, deceives, or gathers information about that Protected Party, except solely through the permitted channels in Section 7. This general prohibition is the controlling term of this notice. The categories below are included without limitation and do not narrow it.

3.1 Surveillance, monitoring, and tracking

All surveillance or monitoring of any Protected Party, by any means, including observation, photography, recording, camera or sensor networks, drones, and third-party access to any camera, account, or network; and all tracking of any Protected Party’s location, movement, communications, or activity, including by any placed tracking device, application, account feature, or vehicle system.

3.2 Investigation

All investigation of any Protected Party by any Person acting outside lawful court process, including profiling, background compilation, dossier-building, pretexting, and inquiries made to any third party about a Protected Party.

3.3 Residence, travel, and transmissions

  1. All activity directed at a Protected Party in or around that Party’s place of residence, and all interference with a Protected Party at home or while traveling in any form — on foot, by private vehicle, by public or commercial transport, or by air. (b) All direction or transmission of any Signal toward a Protected Party, toward a Protected Party’s residence, toward any Device, or toward the Protected Party’s spouse or offspring, and anything else that has any possibility of reaching them, whether directly, indirectly, by reflection, by conduction, or remotely.

3.4 Electronic interference and unauthorized access

All interference with, jamming, or spoofing of any communication, device, or signal; all unauthorized access to, interception of, recording of, or tampering with any Device, account, communication, biometric data, or private location; and all non-consensual testing, experimentation, or exposure of a Protected Party to any substance, device, or procedure.

3.5 Harassment, threats, and presence

All following, watching, appearing at, or driving by any place where a Protected Party lives, works, attends school, worships, or receives care, for any purpose prohibited by this notice; all threats of harm, direct, indirect, coded, or conditional, against any Protected Party, or against their pets, property, employment, or reputation; and all psychological coercion or manipulation by any means.

3.6 Contact on every channel

All Contact with any Protected Party by any channel, including telephone, voicemail, text or multimedia message, email, social media (posts, messages, comments, tags, mentions), any messaging application, postal mail or package, in-person approach, smart-home, doorbell, or vehicle systems, and any future channel or technology. Contact made through intermediaries, proxies, fictitious or anonymous accounts, or spoofed identities is Contact under this notice. Publishing a Protected Party’s private information in order to cause others to make Contact is Contact under this notice.

3.7 Impersonation and synthetic media

All impersonation of any Protected Party, of counsel, of any official or agency, or of any provider or business — in person, in writing, by telephone, or by synthetic, cloned, or artificially generated voice, image, or video.

3.8 Defamation and false reports

All false statements of fact concerning a Protected Party, and all false or bad-faith reports concerning a Protected Party to law enforcement, emergency services, employers, licensing bodies, platforms, or agencies, including fabricated emergency reports.

3.9 Retaliation

All retaliation of any kind against any Protected Party for documenting conduct, preserving evidence, consulting counsel, reporting to authorities, or pursuing any lawful remedy.

3.10 Property and movement

All trespass upon, entry into, or tampering with any Protected Party’s property, vehicles, mail, deliveries, utilities, or security equipment, and all physical obstruction or restraint of a Protected Party’s movement.

4. ATTEMPTS, ASSISTANCE, AND USE OF OTHERS

This notice prohibits attempts and preparations to the same extent as completed acts. No Person shall aid, abet, advise, encourage, hire, counsel, procure, pay, task, direct, or permit any other person, or any device, account, facility, or service under that Person’s control, to engage in any Conduct prohibited by this notice. Directing conduct through another person does not remove it from this notice.

5. PERSONS AND PLACES COVERED

This notice is addressed to every Person, wherever that Person is located and wherever the Conduct originates. Conduct is within this notice if it is directed at, transmitted toward, or reaches any Protected Party, residence, or Device, including Conduct originating outside the State of Georgia. The prohibitions follow the Protected Parties: they apply at every location, in transit, and at every temporary or permanent residence.

6. NO CIRCUMVENTION

The substance of Conduct governs, not its label or form. Relabeling, recharacterizing, disguising, routing, encrypting, splitting into parts, spacing over time, attributing to another source, or carrying out any prohibited Conduct by a new method, technology, intermediary, or pretext does not remove it from this notice. Any reading of this notice that would permit what it prohibits is incorrect.

7. PERMITTED CHANNELS — THE ONLY EXCEPTIONS

The following, and nothing else, are permitted:

  1. Lawful service of process, performed strictly as such by a person legally authorized to perform it.
  2. Written communication through the Protected Party’s licensed attorney of record, addressed to that attorney and to no one else.
  3. A genuine emergency involving imminent danger to life or serious bodily injury, reported to emergency services (911) or law enforcement — never by direct approach or direct contact with a Protected Party.

Use of a permitted channel as a pretext for any prohibited Conduct is itself a violation of this demand. Nothing in this notice asks any person to surrender any right to truthful testimony compelled by law, and nothing in it is directed at communications made for a legitimate purpose through the permitted channels above.

8. PRESERVATION OF EVIDENCE

Litigation and applications for court protection arising from the matters addressed here are contemplated. Every Person who obtains knowledge of this notice shall preserve, intact and unaltered, all recordings, messages, footage, logs, metadata, documents, and records in that Person’s possession, custody, or control that relate to any Protected Party or to any Conduct described in this notice, and shall suspend any automatic deletion of such material. Destruction or alteration after this notice will be documented and may be presented to a court.

9. DO NOT RESPOND

Do not respond to this notice except through a permitted channel in Section 7. Any other response, reply, acknowledgment, or contact will be preserved as evidence of receipt and knowledge of this notice and of the withdrawal of consent it records.

10. DURATION, REVOCATION, AND NO WAIVER

This notice is permanent and continuing. It takes effect as stated above and does not expire by lapse of time. It may be revoked only by a written revocation signed by the Protected Party. No oral statement, silence, delay, lapse in enforcement, or change of circumstance revokes, suspends, waives, or narrows it. Failure to act on any single violation is not permission for that violation or for any other. This notice records a continuing withdrawal of consent; it does not extend, and is not limited by, any statute of limitations applicable to any underlying claim or offense.

11. CONSTRUCTION AND SEVERABILITY

“Including” means “including without limitation.” Headings are for convenience only. If any single provision of this notice is held invalid or unenforceable by a court, that provision shall be read down to the narrowest extent necessary to make it valid, and every remaining provision continues in full force. No provision shall be construed against the Protected Party by reason of authorship.

12. CONSEQUENCES

Conduct described in this notice may violate Georgia and federal law, including, as applicable: O.C.G.A. §§ 16-5-90 (stalking), 16-5-91 (aggravated stalking), 16-11-39.1 (harassing communications), 16-11-62 (eavesdropping and surveillance), 16-11-37 (terroristic threats and acts), 16-4-1 (attempt), and 16-2-20 (party to a crime); and 18 U.S.C. §§ 2261A (stalking by interstate travel or by interstate communications facilities), 875 (interstate threats), 2511 (interception of communications), 2701 (unlawful access to stored communications), 1030 (computer fraud and abuse), and 912 (impersonation of a federal officer), and 47 U.S.C. § 223 (harassing interstate communications). Continued Conduct after this notice will be documented and preserved, and may be reported to law enforcement and prosecutors and presented to a court, including in an application for a stalking protective order under O.C.G.A. § 16-5-94 and for any other relief the law provides. This notice is itself evidence that the Conduct is without consent and that the actor had knowledge of that fact.

ALL RIGHTS ARE RESERVED.
